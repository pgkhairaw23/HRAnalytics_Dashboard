Interactive Dashboard

The dashboard provides interactive navigation through different HR departments:

Human Resources – Click to view HR-related employee and attrition insights.
Research & Development – Click to view R&D employee and attrition analytics.
Sales – Click to view Sales employee and attrition insights.

Users can click on each department tab to dynamically view the corresponding data and visualizations, making it easier to analyze department-wise employee trends and attrition.
Dashboard Features:
📊 Employee Count • 📉 Attrition Analysis • 👥 Gender • 🎓 Education • 🎂 Age • 💰 Salary • 💼 Job Role • ⏳ Experience

Key Insights:
Total Employees: 1,413
Employee Attrition: 229 (16.2%)
Average Salary: 6.52K
Average Experience: 7.04 years
Highest attrition is observed among the 26–35 age group
Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives show higher attrition
Most attrition occurs in the up to 5K salary range
Gender-wise attrition: Male – 145 | Female – 84

Tools/Technologies: Power BI, Data Visualization, HR Analytics
